Who we are

A Gateway to Revival, Restoration & Glory

Gateway Revival Glory Church (GRGC) is a Spirit-filled, Bible-believing church headquartered in Kampala, Uganda, under the visionary leadership of Pastor Agatha Idusso. What began as a small fellowship has grown into a thriving family with branches in Tema Village, Kikyusa and Kayabwe on Masaka Road.

Our mandate is simple: win souls, disciple believers, care for the vulnerable — including former street children now rehabilitated and housed at our Gayaza Youth Home — and release the glory of God into our communities.

  • Passionate worship & undiluted Word of God
  • Powerful prayer, healing & deliverance ministry
  • Children, youth, women & men fellowships
  • Community projects: shelter, feeding, education & skills training

Our Projects & Ministries

“Religion that God accepts is this: to look after orphans and widows in their distress.” — James 1:27

❤ Flagship Project

Gayaza Youth Home — Shelter for Former Street Children

Our beating heart. The Gayaza Youth Home in Gayaza rescues, rehabilitates and houses former street children — giving them a safe home, meals, clothing, counselling, schooling and, above all, the love of Jesus. Many have returned to school; others are learning trades and discovering their God-given purpose.

  • Safe housing, daily meals & medical care
  • School fees, uniforms & scholastic materials
  • Counselling, discipleship & life-skills training
  • Reunification support with traced families where possible
